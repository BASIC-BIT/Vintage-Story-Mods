# The BASICs roleplay

Language for roleplay chat and shared dice rolls.

## Language

**Chat mode**:
The selected reach of a local message: whisper, normal, or yell.
_Avoid_: Volume, chat type

**Chat type**:
The kind of roleplay message, such as in-character speech, emote, local OOC, or global OOC.
_Avoid_: Chat mode, channel

**Roll**:
A dice result generated for a player's expression. An ordinary scene roll follows the player's selected local chat mode regardless of their selected chat type.

**Range marker**:
A visible label identifying the chat mode of a roll, such as (W) for whisper or (Y) for yell. It identifies the roll's in-game reach, including when the result is also viewed in Discord.




**Private roll**:
A roll whose result is shown only to the player who requested it. It has no nearby recipients or Discord copy.

**Roll reason**:
Optional player-authored context explaining what a roll is for. It is separate from the dice expression and does not affect the result.



**Success pool**:
A roll that counts dice meeting a condition instead of summing their faces.

**Exploding die**:
A die that generates an additional roll when its explosion condition is met. Additional rolls contribute according to the expression's mechanics.
