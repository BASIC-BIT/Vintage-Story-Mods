## Scene Markers prerelease

Leave persistent roleplay descriptions in the world. Craft a marker from two stones of the same type, place it on the ground or a wall, then Shift-right-click to write. Right-click to read.

- Customize symbols, colors, title icons, bubble size, and when text appears.
- Mark scenes as read for yourself, or lock your markers against editing and pickup. Unlock before moving them; their text and appearance travel with them.
- Server owners can toggle Scene Markers under `/basic config` → Chat/RP. Enabled by default; changes require a restart.
- Also fixes typing across wrapped lines in character sheets and player notes, and adds configurable blocks that block or allow line of sight.

For Vintage Story 1.22.6. This is a prerelease with final in-game QA still incomplete. The two-stone crafting fix is included. Hand-edited, unusual GUI scales can misplace the description field; use the in-game scale slider.
